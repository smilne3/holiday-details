# Family Wish Lists

One family's wish lists in one place, kept all year: what each person would like, and who is buying what, kept from the person it's for.

## Language

### People

**Household**:
People who live together, adults and kids. Its adults keep its kids' lists and share its gift ideas.
_Avoid_: family (that's everyone in the app)

**Organizer**:
The adult who sets up the households and people, with each person's birthday, and sends each adult their personal link.
_Avoid_: admin

**Personal link**:
The link that lets one adult in, with no password. Kids have none.
_Avoid_: login, account, invitation (the invitation is the email that carries the link)

**List owner**:
The person a wish list is for.
_Avoid_: "owner" on its own, since this repo's docs use "the owner" for the person who runs the app

**Keeper**:
An adult who looks after a kid's list: any adult in the kid's household. On screen, "Kept by Ben & Maya".
_Avoid_: parent, guardian, manager

### Lists

**Item**:
One wish on a list, put there by its list owner or a keeper.
_Avoid_: wish, thing

**Claim**:
A buyer's "I'll buy this" on an item. An item has at most one.
_Avoid_: reservation, pledge

**Off-list gift**:
A gift a buyer adds to someone's list that isn't one of their wishes. Everyone but that person sees it, and who's buying it. It always has its buyer: taking it back deletes it, leaving nothing behind, unlike a removed item.
_Avoid_: surprise (that's the promise below), extra, gift idea (that's a household's private plan, below)

**Gift idea**:
A gift a household is thinking of buying for someone, in the family or not. Only its adults see it, never the person it's for, and it isn't a promise to buy; adding it to a family member's list makes it an off-list gift.
_Avoid_: idea list, plan, off-list gift (that one the family can see)

**Buyer**:
The person who claimed an item, or added an off-list gift. Only the buyer can change or delete an off-list gift.

**Recipient**:
The person a gift is for, seen from its buyer's side: the list owner of a claimed item, or the person an off-list gift was added for.
_Avoid_: giftee

**Removed**:
Said of an item its list owner or a keeper took off the list. A claim on it stays, so its buyer finds out.
_Avoid_: deleted, off-list

**The surprise**:
The app's promise that a list owner never learns what's been claimed, or added off-list, on their own list, or what anyone has as a gift idea for them. Keepers do see claims and off-list gifts on their kids' lists.
_Avoid_: the secret

### Occasions

**Occasion**:
A day the family gives gifts: Christmas, or someone's birthday.
_Avoid_: holiday, event, season

**Cleared**:
Said of a claimed item or off-list gift once the next occasion of the person it's for is over: it comes off the list for everyone, while items nobody claimed stay. A birthday is over the next day; Christmas, and any birthday in December or January, at the end of January.
_Avoid_: given, ended, expired, done
